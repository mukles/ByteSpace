Motion Graphics for Social is an advanced course from Studio Ember for anyone who wants to animate with timing and personality. You will work through short, focused lessons and finish each module with a small project you can keep.

Along the way you will go from simple motion tests to finished shots. By the end you will have a repeatable workflow and the confidence to keep improving on your own.
