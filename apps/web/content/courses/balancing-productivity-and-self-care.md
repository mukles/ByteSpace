Balancing Productivity and Self-Care is a beginner course from PurePearl Studio for anyone who wants to build sustainable work habits. You will work through short, focused lessons and finish each module with a small project you can keep.

Along the way you will set up systems for planning, focus and rest. By the end you will have a repeatable workflow and the confidence to keep improving on your own.
