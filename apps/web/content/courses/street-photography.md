Street Photography is an intermediate course from Studio Ember for anyone who wants to take photos with intention. You will work through short, focused lessons and finish each module with a small project you can keep.

Along the way you will master light, composition and editing on location. By the end you will have a repeatable workflow and the confidence to keep improving on your own.
