UX Research Methods is a beginner course from Pixel & Pine for anyone who wants to design clear, usable interfaces. You will work through short, focused lessons and finish each module with a small project you can keep.

Along the way you will move from research and wireframes to polished, tested screens. By the end you will have a repeatable workflow and the confidence to keep improving on your own.
