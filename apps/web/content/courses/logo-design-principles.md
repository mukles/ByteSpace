Logo Design Principles is an advanced course from Northwind Labs for anyone who wants to create confident, consistent visual work. You will work through short, focused lessons and finish each module with a small project you can keep.

Along the way you will practise layout, type and colour on real briefs. By the end you will have a repeatable workflow and the confidence to keep improving on your own.
