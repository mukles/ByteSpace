Guitar Foundations is an intermediate course from Pixel & Pine for anyone who wants to write, play and produce music with confidence. You will work through short, focused lessons and finish each module with a small project you can keep.

Along the way you will build skills track by track with guided sessions. By the end you will have a repeatable workflow and the confidence to keep improving on your own.
