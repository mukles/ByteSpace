Figure Drawing Fundamentals is an advanced course from Pixel & Pine for anyone who wants to draw and paint with control and expression. You will work through short, focused lessons and finish each module with a small project you can keep.

Along the way you will work from observation to finished pieces. By the end you will have a repeatable workflow and the confidence to keep improving on your own.
